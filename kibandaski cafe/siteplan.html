Local Foodie Blog Site Plan — WDD 131 Dynamic Web Fundamentals

A plan for a community food blog covering local restaurants, seasonal recipes, and food events.

Site Name

Local Foodie Blog Brand

The name reflects a blog built around local food culture — from hidden-gem restaurants to farmer's markets and home cooking. It's memorable and tells visitors exactly what the site is about: "Local" builds community connection, "Foodie" speaks to enthusiasts, and "Blog" sets the right expectation for the content.

Domain: localfoodieblog.com Available

Site Purpose

The Local Foodie Blog gives the community a single place to discover local restaurants, seasonal recipes, and food events, and to swap recommendations through a member forum.

Scenarios

  • Which local restaurant has the best atmosphere for a date night?
  • What can I cook with fresh zucchini from the farmer's market this week?

Color Schema

A warm palette meant to feel fresh, inviting, and community-driven.

#C0392B — Primary (headings, links, borders)
#F1C40F — Accent (buttons, highlights)
#2C3E50 — Dark (secondary headings, text)
#FAF8F5 — Background
#FFFFFF — Cards, form fields

The red draws attention to key elements, the yellow adds warmth to calls-to-action, navy keeps text readable, and the cream background lets food photography stand out.

Typography

  • Headings — Playfair Display: a serif font with elegant strokes, used for h1–h4.
    "The best meals are made with love."
  • Body — Open Sans: a clean sans-serif for comfortable reading in reviews and recipes.
    "Fresh ingredients, bold flavors, and a sense of community."
  • Fallback stack: 'Playfair Display', Georgia, serif for headings, 'Open Sans', Arial, sans-serif for body text.
